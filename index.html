      Positive Emotion Practice

      What if joy could become as familiar as stress?

      Your brain continually builds pathways around what you notice and experience. Stress pathways get a lot of practice. This practice gives your brain more positive experiences to recognize and return to.

      Most people can name a wide range of negative emotions but have far fewer words for the positive feelings available to them. Expanding that vocabulary builds new neural pathways — ones that can begin to override the stressful patterns running on autopilot.

      The practice

      1. Reveal one emotion each day. You don't need to already feel it. You're widening your range of what's possible.
      2. Find it visually. Do a quick image search for the word and scroll until an image stands out. Save it to your photos.
      3. Send the word and the image to your partner. Keep it simple. Sharing the emotion is enough.
      4. Track it through the day. Look for it everywhere — in yourself, in others, in your surroundings. Hold it lightly and see what it opens up.

      Why a partner

      Naming a feeling for another person is how the brain learns to recognize it — and it grows connection with someone who matters to you. All your partner needs to do is receive the word and the image. If they want to, they can send their own positive emotion back. Sometimes people only want to receive; sometimes they want to send. Both are the practice.

      Positive Emotion Practice

      What if joy could become as familiar as stress?

      Your brain builds pathways around what you notice. Stress pathways get a lot of practice. This is practice for the other kind.

      Each day, reveal one positive emotion. Find an image that captures it. Send both to one person. You don't need to feel the emotion first — you're widening your range.

      Sending it to someone is the part that makes it stick. Naming a feeling for another person is how the brain learns to recognize it, while growing connection with someone who matters to you. All your partner needs to do is receive it. If they want to, they can send their own positive emotion back.

      Who's your partner?

      Pick one person — a friend, partner, sibling, colleague. Let them know a word is coming most days. That's all they need to do.
